// About

Frames Commander is a specialized video player designed for animators and VFX artists who need precise frame-by-frame control over their references.

Viewing modes

Split-screen comparison, a 2×2 grid for viewing up to four videos simultaneously, and video layers with adjustable opacity.

Built for animation work

Two-way Maya synchronization, drawing tools for on-frame annotations, professional composition grids, and Overlay Pro mode for floating references over any software.

Free and portable

No installation required.
