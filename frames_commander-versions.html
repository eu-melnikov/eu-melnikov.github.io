Frames Commander

// About

Frames Commander is a specialized video player designed for animators and VFX artists who need precise frame-by-frame control over their references.

Viewing modes

Split-screen comparison, a 2×2 grid for viewing up to four videos simultaneously, video layers with adjustable opacity, and Overlay Pro mode for floating references over any software.

Clips as one sequence

Select several shots in the playlist and review them as a single video, just like in RV: one timeline with the cuts marked on it, frame numbers running straight through, joined audio. Shift+→ and Shift+← jump between cuts, while playback, Loop, In/Out points and scrubbing work across them. Playlist files can be reordered by dragging and sorted by name, and the sequence is saved into the .fcmd project.

Sound you can see

A waveform runs as a strip along the edge of the picture: the playhead moves along it, and hits and syllables are visible at a glance. The wheel zooms into the part you need and sets the In/Out segment, the middle button drags the zoomed piece. Scrub audio plays a short slice of a frame's sound while you move through the video by hand — for lip-sync.

Separate audio track

WAV, MP3 and other files instead of the embedded sound: attach a voice-over or music to a silent playblast without a video editor, with a frame offset. The track is saved in the project and goes into exports.

Text and annotation on the frame

A caption lives on its frame as an object and stays editable at any time: fonts with live preview, styles, outline, backing, shadow, text in a frame that fits its own size, and a "Make it readable" button that picks the look from the frame underneath. Next to it — pencil, vanishing ink, shapes and arrows, and ghosting that shows drawings from nearby frames. Keys 1–8 pick a tool, "-" and "=" change the line width or font size, and every key can be reassigned.

Curve Speed

Speed-ups and slow-downs are set by a curve right in the player. The wheel zooms into a part of the curve, Ctrl+wheel stretches the speed scale, the middle button pans the graph, and a key on the playhead line can be selected, moved and deleted.

Built for animation work

Two-way Maya synchronization, Tracking Mode, professional composition grids and timecode, and export of the In/Out segment as one MP4 with sound or as a JPEG sequence.

Free and portable

No installation required. Settings live in the user profile folder and survive updates. The interface and the manual come in English and Russian.

Version 1.19 grew out of feedback from 1.18 users. Alongside it come scrub audio for lip-sync, hotkeys for every drawing tool, settings that no longer reset, and a new visual HTML user manual.

Clips as one sequence — like in RV

Check the edit without assembling it in a video editor.

  • Select clips in the playlist with Ctrl or Shift and press the As one sequence button at the bottom of the playlist. With fewer than two selected, the whole playlist goes in, in playlist order.
  • One timeline for the whole sequence: cuts show as yellow ticks, frame numbers run straight through, and the current clip's name appears right on the frame (the N button).
  • Shift+→ and Shift+← jump to the next and previous cut. Playback, Loop, In/Out points, scrubbing and scrub audio all work across cuts.
  • Clips can differ: another resolution is letterboxed, a clip without sound plays silence, and variable-frame-rate screencasts stay in sync. A clip with a different fps is conformed to the sequence — the program warns you.
  • Drawings and bookmarks are remembered per clip and its own frame: reorder the clips or open a clip on its own — the annotations stay in place.
  • The sequence is saved into the .fcmd project with the position and segment. If a clip has gone missing from disk, the sequence is rebuilt from the rest.
  • The sequence runs in the Normal mode; Split, 2x2 and other modes close it. Turn the button off and the clip under the playhead stays in the player, on the same frame.

Exporting a sequence as one file

Send the edit for review as a single video, not a pile of files.

  • The regular Export (Ctrl+E) in sequence mode renders the In/Out segment across every cut into one MP4 with the sequence audio.
  • The frames are exactly what the player shows, at the first clip's resolution.
  • A JPEG sequence is numbered by sequence frames.
  • Cancel stops the export and deletes the unfinished file.

Scrub audio for lip-sync

Hear a frame's sound when you move through the video by hand.

  • The sound-wave button next to the volume, or Shift+S: every frame step plays a short slice of that frame's audio.
  • It sounds when stepping with the arrows and dragging on the timeline or right on the frame; normal playback, jumps and bookmark navigation stay as before.
  • Right-click the button to choose the slice length: 1, 2 or 3 frames.
  • Works in Normal, Split and 2x2 modes (the active panel is voiced); the switch is shared and remembered. Off by default.

Hotkeys for drawing

Annotate without trips to the toolbar.

  • 1–8 — pencil, vanishing ink, line, arrow, rectangle, circle, triangle, text. A digit turns drawing on by itself.
  • "-" and "=" — line width or font size.
  • Delete clears the frame's annotation immediately, without a prompt; Shift+Delete clears all frames, with confirmation. Both can be undone with Ctrl+Z.
  • Button tooltips show the current keys; every key can be reassigned on the Hotkeys tab.

Stability and convenience

  • Settings no longer reset. The settings file moved to the user profile folder (%APPDATA%\FramesCommander): it is always writable, even when the program sits in Program Files, and a new version unpacked in place of the old one picks up your hotkeys. Old settings migrate automatically on first launch. A failed read no longer wipes the settings.
  • Per-file bookmarks, just like drawings. Come back to a file in the playlist — its bookmarks are where you left them.
  • Undoing Clear All works again: annotation cleared from every frame comes back with Ctrl+Z.
  • Undo doesn't mix files up: after switching files, the previous file's undo steps are no longer applied to the new one.
  • A new manual. An interactive HTML guide with annotated screenshots, step-by-step instructions, text search and sections for every mode. The Russian edition is ready; the English one is in progress.

The update is recommended for all users of version 1.18.
